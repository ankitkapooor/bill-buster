Small scope. Traceable sources.

This working prototype reviews synthetic, coded outpatient facility bills from Keck Hospital of USC, for services from July 1–September 30, 2026.

It checks possible duplicates, published Medicare unit limits and hospital price context for 20 selected codes. Code-pair checks, scans, real patient documents, physician bills and insurance EOBs are outside this version.

CMS outpatient hospital MUE · 2026 Q3 ↗Keck published prices · file dated April 1, 2026 ↗CMS interpretation and exceptions ↗

Medicare references may not apply to your payer. A price difference is not verified savings or patient responsibility. Findings are questions, not medical, legal or billing advice.

Documents are processed in server memory and are not saved. Refreshing clears your work. This prototype does not use AI or require an API key.
